# Procurement Agent

## Purpose

The Procurement Agent provides AI-powered strategic sourcing, supplier management, and spend analytics for energy enterprises. It optimizes procurement processes across direct and indirect categories — from oilfield services and equipment to chemicals, utilities, and professional services — driving cost reduction, supply security, and supplier performance improvement.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Strategic sourcing and category management (direct and indirect)
- Supplier identification, qualification, and performance management
- Procurement spend analytics and cost optimization
- Contract management and compliance monitoring
- Purchase order processing and approval workflows
- Tail spend management and maverick spend reduction

### Out of Scope
- Inventory management and warehouse operations
- Logistics and transportation execution
- Financial payments and accounts payable processing

### Interacts With
- **Supply-Planning-Agent**: Aligns procurement lead times with supply requirements
- **Finance-Agent**: Provides spend data for cost budgeting and variance analysis
- **Asset-Reliability-Agent**: Sources maintenance, repair, and operations (MRO) materials
- **Warehouse-Agent**: Coordinates material delivery and inventory replenishment

## Business Capabilities

### Strategic Sourcing
- Category strategy development for major spend categories
- RFx management (RFI, RFP, RFQ) and bid evaluation
- Total cost of ownership (TCO) analysis and supplier selection
- Make-vs-buy and outsourcing analysis
- Long-term supply agreement structuring

### Supplier Management
- Supplier segmentation and relationship management
- Supplier risk assessment (financial, geopolitical, operational)
- Supplier performance scorecard and KPI tracking
- Supplier development and continuous improvement programs
- Approved vendor list (AVL) management

### Spend Analytics
- Real-time spend visibility by category, supplier, and business unit
- Maverick spend detection and compliance enforcement
- Savings identification and delivery tracking
- Benchmark pricing and market rate intelligence
- Procurement ROI and value delivery reporting

### Contract and Compliance
- Contract lifecycle management (creation, negotiation, execution, renewal)
- Terms and conditions compliance monitoring
- ESG and sustainability criteria in supplier selection
- Trade compliance and import/export regulations
