# Upstream Operations Agent

## Purpose

The Upstream Operations Agent provides AI-powered advisory and operational intelligence for oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) activities. It supports reservoir management, production optimization, well operations, and field development planning — enabling energy companies to maximize recovery, reduce lifting costs, and safely manage upstream assets.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Reservoir characterization, modelling, and management
- Production optimization across wells, fields, and assets
- Well planning, drilling operations, and completion management
- Field development planning and investment prioritization
- Production surveillance and anomaly detection
- Decommissioning and abandonment planning

### Out of Scope
- Midstream pipeline and gas processing operations
- Refining and downstream processing
- HSE incident management (handled by HSE-Agent)

### Interacts With
- **Asset-Reliability-Agent**: Integrates equipment reliability data with production operations
- **Finance-Agent**: Provides production volumes and cost data for financial planning
- **Supply-Planning-Agent**: Coordinates crude oil availability for refinery feedstock planning
- **HSE-Agent**: Ensures upstream operations comply with safety and environmental regulations

## Business Capabilities

### Reservoir Management
- Reservoir simulation and dynamic modelling
- Material balance analysis and reservoir pressure management
- Enhanced oil recovery (EOR) strategy evaluation
- Production decline analysis and reserves estimation (1P/2P/3P)
- Geological and geophysical data integration

### Production Optimization
- Well performance monitoring and optimization (artificial lift, choke management)
- Field-wide production allocation and balancing
- Gas lift and ESP optimization
- Production loss analysis and opportunity identification
- Real-time SCADA data integration and analytics

### Well and Drilling Operations
- Well planning and drilling programme support
- Well intervention and workover prioritization
- Completion design optimization
- Wellbore integrity management
- Horizontal and directional drilling performance analysis

### Field Development Planning
- Development scenario modelling and economics
- Infill drilling opportunity identification
- Facility capacity planning and debottlenecking
- Capital expenditure optimization across the drilling portfolio
- Production forecasting for business planning
