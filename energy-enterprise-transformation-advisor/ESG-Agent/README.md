# ESG Agent

## Purpose

The ESG Agent provides AI-powered environmental, social, and governance (ESG) compliance, reporting, and strategy support for energy enterprises. It enables sustainability teams and executives to track ESG performance, meet regulatory disclosure requirements, manage carbon emissions, and embed ESG criteria into investment decisions and operational practices — supporting the energy transition and long-term value creation.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions tracking and reporting (Scope 1, 2, and 3)
- ESG regulatory compliance and disclosure (TCFD, GRI, CSRD, ISSB)
- Sustainability strategy development and target-setting (Net Zero, carbon neutrality)
- Social impact and human rights due diligence
- Governance framework and board-level ESG reporting
- Sustainable finance and green bond compliance

### Out of Scope
- HSE incident management and occupational safety (handled by HSE-Agent)
- Detailed financial reporting and audit (handled by Finance-Agent)
- Physical environmental control systems

### Interacts With
- **Finance-Agent**: Provides ESG-linked financial disclosures (TCFD, green finance metrics)
- **HSE-Agent**: Integrates safety and environmental incident data into ESG reporting
- **Corporate-Strategy-Agent**: Aligns ESG strategy with enterprise portfolio and transition planning
- **Procurement-Agent**: Embeds ESG supplier criteria into sourcing decisions

## Business Capabilities

### Emissions Management
- GHG inventory development and emissions factor management
- Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions calculation and reporting
- Carbon footprint attribution by asset, business unit, and product
- Emissions reduction pathway modelling (Net Zero, SBTi targets)
- Carbon offset and credit management

### ESG Reporting and Compliance
- Regulatory framework mapping (TCFD, GRI, CDP, SASB, CSRD, ISSB/IFRS S1/S2)
- Automated ESG data collection, validation, and report generation
- ESG rating agency response management (MSCI, Sustainalytics, CDP score)
- Double materiality assessment
- Integrated annual reporting (financial + sustainability)

### Sustainability Strategy
- ESG materiality assessment and stakeholder engagement
- Science-based targets (SBTi) development and progress tracking
- Just transition planning for workforce and communities
- Biodiversity and natural capital risk assessment
- Climate scenario analysis (IEA pathways, NGFS scenarios)

### Governance and Social
- Board ESG governance framework and committee reporting
- Human rights due diligence and supply chain social risk assessment
- Diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) metrics and reporting
- Community investment and social value measurement
- ESG performance incentive linkage (executive remuneration)
