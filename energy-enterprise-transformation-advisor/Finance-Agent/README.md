# Finance Agent

## Purpose

The Finance Agent provides AI-powered financial planning, analysis, cost management, and reporting for energy enterprises. It enables CFOs and finance teams to deliver accurate financial forecasting, optimize capital allocation, manage cost performance, and produce regulatory and management reporting — across all business units, assets, and geographies.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Financial planning and analysis (FP&A): budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis
- Management reporting and performance dashboards
- Cost management and profitability analysis
- Capital expenditure (CAPEX) planning and tracking
- Cash flow forecasting and treasury support
- Regulatory and statutory financial reporting

### Out of Scope
- Commodity trading P&L execution (handled by Sales-Trading-Agent)
- Physical procurement and accounts payable processing
- Operational metrics and production reporting (handled by domain-specific agents)

### Interacts With
- **Corporate-Strategy-Agent**: Provides financial models to support strategic scenario analysis
- **Transformation-PMO-Agent**: Tracks financial benefits realization from transformation initiatives
- **Trading-Risk-Agent**: Integrates risk exposure data for treasury and hedging cost reporting
- **Procurement-Agent**: Provides spend analytics for cost management and budget variance

## Business Capabilities

### Financial Planning and Analysis
- Annual budget development and rolling forecast process
- Multi-year long-range financial planning (LRP)
- Variance analysis (actuals vs. budget vs. forecast) with root cause investigation
- Scenario modelling for commodity price, FX, and volume sensitivities
- Business unit and product line P&L management

### Cost Management
- Cost structure analysis (fixed, variable, direct, indirect)
- Cost reduction opportunity identification and tracking
- Activity-based costing for operational and project cost allocation
- Benchmark cost performance against peers and industry standards
- Working capital optimization (receivables, payables, inventory)

### Capital Management
- CAPEX request evaluation and portfolio prioritization
- Project financial modelling (NPV, IRR, payback period)
- CAPEX vs. OPEX trade-off analysis
- Asset impairment and write-down analysis
- Return on invested capital (ROIC) and economic value added (EVA) tracking

### Reporting and Compliance
- Management accounts and board-level financial reporting
- IFRS/GAAP statutory financial statement preparation support
- Tax provision and deferred tax analysis
- Audit support and internal control documentation
- ESG-linked financial disclosure and reporting (TCFD, GRI)
