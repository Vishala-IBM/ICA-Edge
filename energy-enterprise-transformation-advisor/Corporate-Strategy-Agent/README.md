# Corporate Strategy Agent

## Purpose

The Corporate Strategy Agent provides AI-powered strategic advisory for energy enterprise leadership, enabling scenario-driven portfolio analysis, M&A evaluation, and long-term strategic planning. It synthesizes market intelligence, financial performance, and competitive dynamics to support C-suite decision-making across the enterprise.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Corporate portfolio strategy and capital allocation optimization
- Mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, and joint venture analysis
- Long-range planning (LRP) and strategic scenario modeling
- Competitive intelligence and market positioning
- Business unit performance benchmarking
- Board-level reporting and strategic narrative development

### Out of Scope
- Operational execution and project management
- Day-to-day financial reporting and accounting
- Individual asset-level optimization (handled by Operations agents)

### Interacts With
- **Finance-Agent**: Capital structure, financial modelling, and investment returns
- **Commercial-Marketing-Agent**: Market share analysis and commercial strategy alignment
- **Transformation-PMO-Agent**: Strategy-to-execution bridging and initiative tracking
- **Trading-Risk-Agent**: Portfolio risk exposure and strategic hedging

## Business Capabilities

### Portfolio Management
- Multi-asset portfolio valuation and optimization
- Capital allocation framework development (E&P, refining, renewables)
- Scenario planning for energy transition (net-zero pathways)
- Asset entry/exit analysis and timing recommendations

### M&A and Corporate Development
- Target screening and strategic fit assessment
- Synergy quantification and integration planning
- Deal structuring and valuation (DCF, comparables, precedent transactions)
- Post-merger integration (PMI) roadmap development

### Strategic Planning
- Long-range plan (LRP) development and facilitation
- Business model innovation for energy transition
- Competitive benchmarking against global energy majors
- Strategic KPI definition and performance tracking

### Market Intelligence
- Macro energy market trend analysis (oil price, gas, power, LNG)
- Regulatory and policy landscape monitoring (carbon pricing, energy policy)
- Competitor strategy surveillance and response planning
- Geopolitical risk assessment for international operations
