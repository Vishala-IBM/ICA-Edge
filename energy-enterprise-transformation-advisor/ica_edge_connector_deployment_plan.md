# ICA Edge Connector Deployment Plan

**Connector Readiness:** Fixture and mock connectors are ready to configure for isolated testing, subject to runtime/harness availability. SAP, IBP, Datasphere/BDC, enterprise knowledge and event/API connections are candidates only; no deployed endpoint or production credential is established.

**Deployment Risks:** Unapproved schemas or source ownership; missing identity/ACL and secrets controls; stale or synthetic data mistaken for live; unresolved keys/UoM/calendar; non-idempotent retries/events; direct ERP access or writes bypassing controls. Keep all enterprise write paths disabled.

| Connector | Purpose | Data Source | Authentication Method | Deployment Order | Validation Steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CSV/JSON fixture loader | Read-only, deterministic inputs for agent and dashboard tests. | Versioned synthetic files/snapshots. | Mock test identity; no production secrets. | P1, after runtime and harness foundation. | Validate schema/version, source/as-of, grain, keys, UoM, repeatability and read-only behavior. |
| Master/reference snapshot adapter | Resolve canonical entities and planning references. | Approved Customer, Product, Region, Plant, Facility, Warehouse, Supplier, Asset, Business Unit, calendar and UoM snapshots. | Mock roles in development; least-privilege service identity for approved sources. | P1, with fixture/file adapters. | Check key uniqueness, explicit crosswalks, effective dates, mapping coverage and denied-scope behavior; keep Material-master gap visible. |
| Schema/quality and catalog/lineage adapter | Validate contracts, quality rules and source provenance. | Versioned schemas, fixture records, catalog and lineage metadata. | Scoped service identity; audit tool invocation. | P1, before downstream data consumers. | Test valid/invalid schema, missing key/unit, duplicates, stale data, lineage completeness and actionable failure status. |
| Knowledge retrieval connector | Permission-aware retrieval and citation resolution. | Local/mock document index and approved corpus with version/ACL metadata. | Mock identity/roles initially; propagate caller identity and ACL; enterprise identity only after approval. | P2, after identity/ACL foundation. | Verify ACL filtering before retrieval, citation resolution, stale/conflict warnings, no-result abstention and no restricted leakage. |
| Architecture inventory adapter | Supply evidence for architecture assessment and ADR drafts. | Fixture application, integration and technology-standard inventories; later approved customer inventory. | Mock roles for fixtures; least-privilege, source-authorized identity for enterprise inventory. | P2, after file/schema controls. | Validate source/version/freshness, fact-versus-assumption labeling, unknown-state handling and access denial. |
| Demand Planning source/forecast adapter | Read demand inputs and exchange versioned forecast results. | Demand/sales/scenario fixtures; later approved history or IBP/SD sources. | Mock identity for fixtures; approved SSO/scoped API identity for non-production. | P3, after dictionary and period/UoM/scenario semantics pass. | Validate contract, keys/grain/UoM, source/as-of, approved forecast version, idempotency and missing-actual behavior. |
| Procurement Plan and Asset Status mock/event adapter | Supply planning dependency payloads for isolated workflow tests. | Versioned mock Procurement Plan and Asset Status events. | Mock producer/consumer roles; no ERP credentials. | P4, after Demand contract and Material/UoM mapping. | Test schema, producer/source, timestamps/validity, site/material mapping, stale/conflicting payloads, duplicate/replay and timeout behavior. |
| Governed database/semantic-view connector | Provide approved read-only curated facts at scale. | Selected Datasphere/BDC semantic views or approved SQL/warehouse views. | Non-production SSO/scoped service identity, secrets manager and least privilege. | P5, only after owner, architecture and security approval. | Reconcile to approved source reports; test row/column ACL, freshness, rate/timeout handling, lineage and no-write permissions. |
| SAP released API connector | Read approved S/4 CDS/OData, ECC/BW extracts or IBP planning data. | Customer-approved SAP landscape and released API/extracts. | Approved non-production identity, scoped API authorization and managed secrets; never shared ERP credentials. | P5, after landscape/API, security and reconciliation gates. | Confirm release/API authorization, schema/version, reconciliation, rate limits, audit, errors and read-only enforcement. |
| Integration Suite/API Management/Event Mesh | Later governed API/event handoffs between approved producers and consumers. | Approved versioned contracts and customer-selected integration endpoints. | Approved service identities/secrets, ACL and audited authorization. | P5, after contracts, owners and operational design are approved. | Test correlation IDs, schema compatibility, idempotency, retry/dead-letter, ordering, timeout, replay and downstream postconditions. |
