# SKILL: Energy Enterprise Transformation Advisor

## Skill Name
`energy-enterprise-transformation-advisor`

## Description
This skill enables AI-powered enterprise transformation advisory for energy companies. It orchestrates 19 specialized domain agents spanning the full energy value chain — from upstream exploration and production to downstream refining, trading, and distribution — providing integrated, AI-driven decision support across all business functions.

## Applicable Domains
- Oil & Gas (upstream, midstream, downstream)
- Power Generation & Utilities
- Renewable Energy
- Energy Trading & Marketing
- Petrochemicals

## Core Capabilities

### Strategic Advisory
- Enterprise portfolio analysis and strategic scenario modeling
- M&A target identification and integration planning
- Capital allocation optimization across assets and business units

### Operational Excellence
- Integrated planning across supply chain, operations, and logistics
- Asset reliability and predictive maintenance
- Refinery yield and throughput optimization
- Upstream production optimization and reservoir management

### Commercial & Trading
- Commodity pricing and market intelligence
- Trading risk quantification and hedging strategy
- Commercial deal structuring and margin optimization

### Finance & Compliance
- Financial planning, budgeting, and variance analysis
- ESG reporting and regulatory compliance
- HSE risk management and incident analysis

### Digital & Analytics
- Enterprise data product development
- AI/ML model deployment for operational use cases
- Architecture standards and integration blueprints
- Knowledge management and lessons learned capture

Each agent can be invoked independently or as part of an orchestrated workflow. The advisor routes queries to the appropriate domain agent(s) based on context and question type.
