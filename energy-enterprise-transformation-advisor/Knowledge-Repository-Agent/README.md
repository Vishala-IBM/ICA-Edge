# Knowledge Repository Agent

## Purpose

The Knowledge Repository Agent provides AI-powered institutional knowledge management, document intelligence, and lessons-learned capture for energy enterprises. It enables organizations to systematically capture, organize, search, and retrieve tacit and explicit knowledge — from engineering documents and operating procedures to project lessons learned and expert insights — preventing knowledge loss, accelerating decision-making, and building organizational learning capability.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Knowledge capture and structuring (documents, procedures, lessons learned, expert insights)
- Enterprise document management and intelligent search
- Lessons learned database management and knowledge reuse
- Technical standards and procedure governance
- Knowledge taxonomy and ontology management
- Expert locator and communities of practice support

### Out of Scope
- IT document storage infrastructure and file system management
- Legal contract management and compliance
- Financial records management and audit documentation

### Interacts With
- **Data-Analytics-Agent**: Provides unstructured data and document corpus for NLP and AI analytics
- **Enterprise-Architecture-Agent**: Maintains architecture decision records and design patterns
- **Transformation-PMO-Agent**: Captures transformation lessons learned and best practices
- **All Domain Agents**: Acts as the shared knowledge base for institutional memory across all 19 agents

## Business Capabilities

### Knowledge Capture and Structuring
- Structured lessons-learned capture templates (project, operational, incident)
- Tacit knowledge elicitation from subject matter experts
- Document classification and metadata tagging
- Knowledge contribution workflows and quality review
- Version control and knowledge lifecycle management

### Intelligent Search and Retrieval
- AI-powered semantic search across the enterprise knowledge base
- Natural language query processing for knowledge retrieval
- Contextual recommendation engine ("people also searched for...")
- Cross-domain knowledge linking and related content surfacing
- Knowledge graph visualization for connected concepts

### Document Management and Governance
- Technical document repository (engineering drawings, SOPs, specifications, manuals)
- Document review and approval workflows
- Regulatory and standards compliance tracking
- Document retention policies and archiving management
- Multi-format ingestion (PDF, Word, CAD, email, video)

### Organizational Learning
- Lessons-learned database with structured tagging (project type, discipline, outcome)
- Best practice identification and propagation across business units
- Post-project review facilitation and output capture
- Communities of practice setup and knowledge-sharing facilitation
- Knowledge gap analysis and targeted content development
- Training and onboarding content curation from institutional knowledge
