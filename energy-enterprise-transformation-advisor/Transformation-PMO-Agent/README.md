# Transformation PMO Agent

## Purpose

The Transformation PMO Agent provides AI-powered programme management, transformation governance, and benefits realization tracking for energy enterprise transformation initiatives. It enables transformation leaders and PMOs to plan, execute, and monitor large-scale change programmes — from digital transformation and operational excellence to energy transition and M&A integration — ensuring initiatives deliver on time, on budget, and with measurable business value.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Transformation programme planning and governance
- Initiative portfolio management and prioritization
- Milestone tracking and delivery performance monitoring
- Benefits realization management and value tracking
- Change management and stakeholder engagement
- Transformation reporting and executive communications

### Out of Scope
- Day-to-day operational project management
- IT system development and software delivery
- Financial accounting and payment processing

### Interacts With
- **Corporate-Strategy-Agent**: Translates strategic priorities into executable transformation initiatives
- **Finance-Agent**: Tracks financial investment and benefits realization against transformation business cases
- **Enterprise-Architecture-Agent**: Aligns technology transformation workstreams with architecture roadmaps
- **Knowledge-Repository-Agent**: Captures lessons learned and transformation knowledge for future reuse

## Business Capabilities

### Programme Planning and Governance
- Transformation programme structure design (workstreams, phases, milestones)
- Integrated master plan (IMP) development and maintenance
- Risk and issue management register
- Governance framework design (steering committees, decision escalation)
- Dependency mapping and critical path analysis

### Portfolio Management
- Initiative prioritization using value-effort scoring
- Portfolio health assessment and status reporting (RAG dashboard)
- Resource allocation and capacity planning across initiatives
- Portfolio rebalancing and initiative acceleration/de-scoping
- Benefits case tracking from business case to delivery

### Benefits Realization
- Benefits register development and value mapping
- Leading and lagging KPI definition for transformation benefits
- Value realization milestone tracking and reporting
- Post-implementation review (PIR) facilitation
- Transformation ROI and payback period analysis

### Change Management
- Stakeholder analysis and engagement planning
- Change impact assessment for business units and roles
- Communication planning and delivery
- Organizational readiness assessment
- Training needs analysis and capability building coordination
