# Supply Planning Agent

## Purpose

The Supply Planning Agent provides AI-driven integrated supply chain planning for energy enterprises. It balances supply availability, inventory levels, and demand requirements across the full energy value chain — from crude procurement and refinery feedstock planning to product distribution and customer delivery — ensuring continuity of supply at optimal cost.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Integrated supply chain planning (crude, feedstocks, refined products, gas, LNG)
- Inventory optimization across the supply chain network
- Supply and demand balancing and constraint management
- Sales and operations planning (S&OP) supply side support
- Supply network design and capacity planning
- Distribution requirements planning (DRP)

### Out of Scope
- Physical logistics and transportation execution
- Procurement sourcing and supplier negotiation
- Financial accounting and cost posting

### Interacts With
- **Demand-Planning-Agent**: Receives demand forecasts to drive supply plans
- **Procurement-Agent**: Aligns material requirements with sourcing timelines
- **Logistics-Agent**: Coordinates transport plans with supply schedules
- **Warehouse-Agent**: Manages inventory positioning and storage allocation
- **Refining-Operations-Agent**: Integrates refinery production plans with supply requirements

## Business Capabilities

### Integrated Supply Planning
- Multi-echelon supply network planning (production, distribution, retail)
- Supply constraint identification and resolution
- Crude and feedstock blend optimization for refineries
- Product supply scheduling across pipelines, terminals, and depots

### Inventory Management
- Safety stock optimization by node and product
- Inventory target setting (min/max, cycle stock, buffer stock)
- Inventory visibility and real-time stock position tracking
- Slow-moving and obsolete stock (SLOB) management

### S&OP and Supply Balancing
- Unconstrained and constrained supply plan development
- Demand-supply gap analysis and reconciliation
- Supply scenario planning (disruption, outage, demand surge)
- Consensus supply plan coordination across business units

### Supply Chain Performance
- Supply reliability and service level KPI tracking (OTIF, fill rate)
- Supply chain risk identification and contingency planning
- Carbon footprint and ESG impact of supply chain decisions
- Continuous improvement and supply chain maturity assessment
