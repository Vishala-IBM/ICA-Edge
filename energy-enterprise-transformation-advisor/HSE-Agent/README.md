# HSE Agent

## Purpose

The HSE Agent provides AI-powered health, safety, and environmental (HSE) management for energy enterprises. It enables HSE professionals and operations leaders to identify and mitigate risks, manage regulatory compliance, prevent incidents, and foster a safety culture — protecting people, assets, communities, and the environment across all energy operations.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Process safety and hazard identification (HAZID, HAZOP)
- Occupational health and safety management
- Environmental management and permit compliance
- Incident reporting, investigation, and root cause analysis
- Safety performance monitoring and leading/lagging indicators
- Emergency response planning and crisis management

### Out of Scope
- ESG sustainability reporting and carbon strategy (handled by ESG-Agent)
- Asset integrity and reliability engineering (handled by Asset-Reliability-Agent)
- Environmental, social, and governance investor reporting

### Interacts With
- **Asset-Reliability-Agent**: Integrates equipment integrity data with process safety risk assessment
- **ESG-Agent**: Shares environmental incident and compliance data for ESG disclosure
- **Upstream-Operations-Agent**: Ensures upstream field operations comply with HSE requirements
- **Logistics-Agent**: Ensures dangerous goods transport meets safety and regulatory standards

## Business Capabilities

### Process Safety
- Hazard identification and risk assessment (HAZID, HAZOP, What-If, LOPA)
- Bow-tie analysis for major accident hazard (MAH) scenarios
- Safety instrumented system (SIS) and safety integrity level (SIL) management
- Management of change (MOC) process and safety review
- Process safety performance indicators (leading and lagging) tracking

### Occupational Health and Safety
- Near-miss, incident, and observation reporting and trending
- Risk assessment and job safety analysis (JSA/JSA) management
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) compliance tracking
- Health surveillance and occupational hygiene monitoring
- Safety culture assessment and behavioural-based safety (BBS) programmes

### Environmental Management
- Environmental permit and regulatory compliance monitoring
- Air emissions, effluent discharge, and waste management tracking
- Spill response and environmental incident management
- Environmental impact assessment (EIA) support
- Biodiversity and ecological risk management

### Emergency Preparedness and Crisis Management
- Emergency response plan (ERP) development and maintenance
- Emergency drill planning, execution, and effectiveness assessment
- Incident command structure and crisis communication management
- Mutual aid and industry emergency response coordination
- Business continuity planning for HSE-related disruptions
