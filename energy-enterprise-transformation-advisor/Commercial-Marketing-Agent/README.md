# Commercial Marketing Agent

## Purpose

The Commercial Marketing Agent delivers AI-driven market intelligence, pricing optimization, and commercial strategy support for energy businesses. It enables revenue maximization through demand sensing, pricing analytics, customer segmentation, and channel optimization across all energy product lines and geographies.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Market intelligence and competitive pricing analysis
- Customer segmentation and commercial targeting
- Product pricing strategy (crude, refined products, gas, LNG, power)
- Commercial contract analysis and negotiation support
- Revenue optimization and margin improvement
- Brand and go-to-market strategy for energy products

### Out of Scope
- Physical commodity trading execution (handled by Sales-Trading-Agent)
- Supply chain and logistics operations
- Financial risk hedging and exposure management

### Interacts With
- **Corporate-Strategy-Agent**: Aligns commercial strategy with enterprise portfolio objectives
- **Sales-Trading-Agent**: Translates market intelligence into trading and deal opportunities
- **Demand-Planning-Agent**: Shares demand signals and customer insight for forecasting
- **Finance-Agent**: Margin analysis, revenue reporting, and commercial P&L

## Business Capabilities

### Market Intelligence
- Real-time energy commodity price tracking and trend analysis
- Competitor pricing surveillance and benchmarking
- Market share analysis across products, regions, and customer segments
- Macro demand-supply balance monitoring (oil, gas, power, LNG)

### Pricing Strategy
- Dynamic pricing model development and optimization
- Price-volume trade-off analysis and elasticity modelling
- Spot vs. term contract optimization
- Margin analysis by product, region, and customer segment

### Customer and Commercial Management
- Customer segmentation and lifetime value analysis
- Commercial proposal development and contract structuring
- Customer retention analytics and churn risk identification
- New market entry analysis and channel strategy

### Go-to-Market and Growth
- Product portfolio and bundling strategy
- Digital channel optimization for energy product sales
- Revenue growth management (RGM) framework deployment
- Commercial KPI dashboard and performance tracking
