## Enterprise KPI Model

### Executive Summary
This document outlines the enterprise KPI model for the Energy Enterprise Transformation Advisor repository, derived from existing documentation and datasets.

### KPI Catalog
| KPI | Description | Industry Alignment |
|-----|-------------|---------------------|
| Revenue Growth Rate | YoY revenue change | Energy |
| Customer Satisfaction | Measure of customer satisfaction | Energy |
| Asset Utilization | Percentage of assets in use | Energy |

### Strategic KPIs
| KPI | Target | Owner |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Revenue Growth Rate | 10% YoY | Corporate Strategy Agent |
| ROA | 15% | Finance Agent |

### Operational KPIs
| KPI | Target | Owner |
|-----|--------|-------|

### Executive KPIs

### KPI Ownership Matrix
| KPI | Primary Owner | Secondary Owner(s) |
|-----|---------------|--------------------|
| Revenue Growth Rate | Corporate Strategy Agent | Finance Agent |

### KPI Formulas
| KPI | Formula |
|-----|---------|
| Revenue Growth Rate | ((Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue) * 100 |
| Customer Satisfaction | (Number of Satisfied Customers / Total Customers) * 100 |

### KPI Rollup Hierarchy
- Executive KPIs
  - Strategic KPIs
    - Operational KPIs
